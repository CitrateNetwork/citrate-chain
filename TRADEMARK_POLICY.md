# Trademark Policy

This document describes the intended trademark and brand-use policy for Citrate.

## Reserved Marks

Unless explicitly licensed in writing by Citrate Inc., the following names and brand assets are reserved:

- Citrate
- Citrate OpenWallet
- SALT
- Citrate logos, icons, marks, wordmarks, and product branding
- official app and docs presentation that identifies a product as the canonical Citrate deployment

## What Forks May Do

Forks may:

- study the code
- modify the code
- run private or educational networks
- describe themselves as “based on Citrate” in factual terms

## What Forks May Not Do Without Permission

Forks may not:

- call themselves the official Citrate network
- call themselves Citrate OpenWallet
- use Citrate logos or visual identity in a way that implies endorsement
- market themselves as the canonical Citrate deployment
- present a commercial derivative under Citrate branding

## Recommended Fork Behavior

If you fork Citrate:

- choose a distinct project name
- replace marks, logos, and brand assets
- state clearly that your deployment is an independent fork
- avoid confusion with the official network, wallet, SDKs, and docs
